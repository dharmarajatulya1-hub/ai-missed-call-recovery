SMS Opt-In & Consent

Svaraa's business-owner customers opt in to SMS notifications during signup on our homepage. Below is the exact consent shown next to the mobile-number field on the signup form. The box is not pre-checked — the user must actively check it to opt in, and consent is not required to purchase.

What the consent includes
  • Message type: account, lead, and booking notifications
  • Message frequency: recurring; varies with call activity
  • Rates disclaimer: "Msg & data rates may apply"
  • Opt-out: reply STOP to unsubscribe
  • Help: reply HELP for assistance
  • Links: Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
  • Submit: "Start free trial" button
  • Not pre-checked: user actively selects the box

Recipients of these messages are Svaraa's own subscribing business owners (account holders), not their end callers. Mobile opt-in data is never shared with third parties for marketing — see our Privacy Policy.
